About Me

Aspiring Marine Conservationist

My name is Zale Leow, a Secondary 4 student from Beatty Secondary School. Since young, I have had a strong fascination with the sea. It began with a simple book for children at 3 about a hundred facts about the deep sea. From there, my love for nature developed. I frequently visited aquariums and zoos both in and out of Singapore, but it was rather passive until last year. Inspired by 'The Blue Planet', I wanted to be more active towards nature conservation. Through a perfect opportunity in the 41st National Bird Race, I have since taken up a hobby of Birdwatching, as well as contributing in various other sectors.

Animal Conservation

Since young, I have always been passionate about nature, especially systems. However, I never really took the time to think about it. That was, until my trip to Punalu'u Beach in Hawaii, where I not only was amazed by the black sand, but also by the sight of a critically endangered hawksbill turte coming up to nest! I soon found out the correlation, as they needed the black volcanic sand for the heat. However, that wasn't the last time I encountered one, as merely a year later as part of my NEXUS@Beatty 3015 Bali Exchange, where we helped conserve the very same Hawksbill turtles who swam all the way to Saba Beach's black sands at Saba Asri Turtles Conservation centre.

Wildlife & Population Dynamics

Studying wildlife, especially the dynamics with humans, as always been interesting to me. Due to exterior factors such as climate change and even Singapore's status quo as a 'City in a Garden', leading to many wildlife-human interactions. I have picked up Birdwatching as a way to channel and passively observe animals in a fun and accessible manner.
